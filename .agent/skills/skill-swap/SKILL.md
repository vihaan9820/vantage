# SkillSwap Master Platform Skill

## Subsystems

1. **2-Way Barter Matchmaker**
   - Reciprocal matching algorithm based on teaching capabilities vs learning wishlists.
   - Live compatibility score calculations (`calculateMatchScore`).
   - Role swapping (`⇄`) and instant filter chips.

2. **TimeBank Credit Economy**
   - 1-hour = 1 credit barter ledger (`/wallet`).

   - Karma and reputation scoring.

3. **1:1 Live Video Rooms**
   - In-app WebRTC video modal with interactive scratchpad notes, camera/mic controls, and barter term HUD.

4. **UI/UX Pro Max Standards**
   - Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`) & Reciprocity Emerald (`#10B981`) colorway.
   - Glassmorphic cards with specular highlights.
   - Anime.js and Framer Motion micro-interactions.
